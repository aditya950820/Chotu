Counting vs Learning

Left: the no-gradient model. Never trained. For every next word it finds the longest stretch of the text so far that also appears in 300M tokens of our training data, counts what followed it there, and mixes that with words already used in the answer. Right: the 700M twin, a 7M-parameter network trained by gradient descent. Same prompt, same settings, same random seed. These answers were produced by both models on 16 Nov; pick a prompt and a setting to compare.
