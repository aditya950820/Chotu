Why 256, and why that's not wasteful

256 × 2,048

Chotu asks 256 windows of 2,048 tokens before each stride: 524,288 tokens per stride. Over 10.06 billion tokens that makes 19,181 strides. Asking 64 instead would give about 77,000 shakier strides; asking 1,024 would give about 4,800 very steady ones.

a megaphone

A GPU doesn't ask strangers one at a time. It asks a whole group at once, like using a megaphone, so reading 32 windows together takes far less than 32 times as long as reading one. Small crowds leave most of the H100 idle. That's why the H100 asks 32 at a time, 8 rounds in a row, then takes one stride.
