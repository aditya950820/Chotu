## Short Q&A
1. What causes the seasons on Earth?
2. Why do we need to sleep?
3. What is the difference between weather and climate?
4. How does a vaccine protect the body?

## Summaries
5. Summarise in two sentences: "The Industrial Revolution began in Britain in the late 1700s. Machines replaced hand tools, factories grew in cities, and people moved from farms to towns for work. It changed how goods were made and how people lived."
6. Give the main idea of this text in one line: "Bees pollinate many crops. Without them, fruits and vegetables would be harder and more expensive to grow."
7. Summarise the story of a tortoise and a hare racing, in three sentences.
8. What is the key point of this advice: "Save a little money every month, even when it seems too small to matter."

## Rewriting
9. Rewrite more politely: "Send me the report now."
10. Rewrite in simple words for a 10-year-old: "Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy."
11. Fix the grammar: "She don't like going to school on monday."
12. Make this sentence shorter: "Due to the fact that it was raining heavily, we made the decision to stay inside."

## Simple instructions
13. List three fruits that are red.
14. Write a two-line poem about the sea.
15. Give me three tips for studying for an exam.
16. Count from 5 to 10.

## Chat
17. Hi! How are you today?
18. I'm feeling nervous about a job interview tomorrow. Any advice?
19. Can you recommend a hobby I can start at home?
20. What's a good name for a pet cat?
