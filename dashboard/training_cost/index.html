What each size assumes

Chotu uses its real plan. The 7B and 100B models read the usual compute-efficient amount of text for their size; many real models read far more, which raises the cost in proportion. MFU is lower for Chotu because small models leave the GPU partly idle.

What these numbers leave out

  • Storage for data and checkpoints, and GST. The portal total had no storage entered.
  • The LR test, smaller experiments and restarts. Real projects often spend several times the final run on these.
  • Availability. The portal lists single-card instances; 64 or 1,024 linked cards for the bigger models is a separate booking, and it may not exist at these prices.
  • Fast links between cards. Multi-card training needs them; the L4, Trainium and TPU rows are least likely to scale the way this sheet assumes.
  • Real speed. Every card is rated at the same MFU here. On Chotu, big cards like the B200 likely run below it, which erases their small paper advantage.
  • People. The engineers who build and babysit a 100B run cost more than the GPUs for anything smaller.
